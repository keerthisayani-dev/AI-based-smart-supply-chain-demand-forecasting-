About This Project

DemandIQ is an AI-assisted demand forecasting system for retail inventory planning. It predicts upcoming demand using historical sales trends and provides operational insights for stocking, reorder planning, and performance tracking.

Primary Goal
Demand Forecast
Scope
Category-Level
Output Horizon
Daily/Weekly/Monthly

How The Project Works

  • Loads historical retail records and aggregates time-series demand by category.
  • Trains forecast models on cleaned demand signals.
  • Generates future demand predictions for a chosen date range.
  • Compares forecast and recent actuals through charts and tabular outputs.
  • Supports admin-controlled retraining and dataset upload workflows.

Data Used By The Model

Current dataset: Dataset/retail_store_inventory.csv

Date & Identity
date, store id, product id, category, location
Inventory & Demand
inventory level, units sold, units ordered, demand forecast
Pricing Signals
price, discount, competitor pricing
External Factors
weather condition, holiday/promotion, seasonality

How To Measure "How Much Better"

Improvement should be measured against a baseline method (for example, previous-month average or simple moving average), using the same period.

  • Track forecast error metrics: MAPE, MAE, and RMSE.
  • Compare stock-out and overstock incidents before vs after using forecasts.
  • Track planning impact: reorder timing quality and service-level stability.

In this project, the "better" value is determined by lower forecast error and improved inventory decisions over historical operation.

Explainable AI (XAI) Module

Many AI models are black boxes. This module explains why demand predictions move up or down so managers can trust operational decisions.

  • SHAP values: shows feature-level contribution to the next forecasted demand value.
  • Feature importance: ranks model drivers using coefficient magnitude.
  • Forecast explanation dashboard: summarizes top positive/negative drivers with clear tables.

Explainable models help managers understand why demand predictions change.

Project Overview

Project Overview Branch

DemandIQ is an AI-assisted retail demand prediction and analytics system designed for inventory planning and operational decision support. It combines predicted reporting with past demand review, strategy simulation, dataset editing, platform comparison, recent history tracking, and admin upload workflows in one dashboard.

Primary Goal
Demand Planning
Scope
City, Store, Product
Output Horizon
Predicted And Analysis Views

Workflow Sub-Branch

  • Loads cleaned retail inventory and sales data across city, store, category, and product dimensions.
  • Builds predicted outputs for a selected business scope and date window using historical demand signals.
  • Shows predicted results, past demand view, inventory insights, profitability metrics, and recent history in separate analytics pages.
  • Supports demand strategy simulation for price and discount changes on selected products and platforms.
  • Includes dynamic dataset editing, platform comparison, and admin-controlled upload and retraining workflows.

Data Foundation Sub-Branch

Current dataset: Dataset/retail_store_inventory_city_level_cleaned.csv

Target Variable: units sold

Date & Identity
date, store id, product id, category, location, region, city, state, country, store_name
Inventory & Demand
inventory level, units sold, units ordered, demand predicted
Pricing Signals
price, discount, competitor pricing
External Factors
weather condition, holiday/promotion, seasonality

Performance Review Sub-Branch

Improvement should be measured against a baseline planning method using the same category, store, and time period.

  • Track predicted error metrics: MAPE, MAE, and RMSE.
  • Compare stock-out and overstock incidents before and after using predicted-driven planning.
  • Track planning impact through reorder timing, stock coverage, and service-level stability.
  • Measure business lift from strategy simulation and comparison outputs such as demand change, revenue change, and profit impact.

In this project, "better" means lower predicted error, faster planning decisions, and stronger inventory and revenue outcomes.

Analytics Branch Modules

The current dashboard is organized into focused analytics pages that open from the sidebar so users can move quickly between predicted views and operational analysis.

  • Predicted Results: demand chart, predicted table, inventory insights, and profitability metrics.
  • Past Demand View: recent actual demand review for the selected scope.
  • Demand Strategy: simulation page for price and discount changes with demand and revenue impact.
  • Dynamic Dataset Editor: load, edit, add, and delete product-level records.
  • Compare Platforms: side-by-side comparison of the same product across platforms.
  • Recent History and Admin Upload: historical result tracking plus admin data management workflows.

These modules help managers move from predicted insights to action without leaving the reporting environment.
